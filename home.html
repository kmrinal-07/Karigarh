OUR STORY | SINCE 2008

A Small Beginning, A Beautiful Journey

It All Started With a Dream

Every remarkable journey begins with a simple idea. For KARIGAR, it was the dream of bringing people closer to the authentic flavours of India.

Established in 2008, KARIGAR began as a small restaurant with a big passion for traditional Indian food. Our early journey was built around a simple promise: serve delicious food, respect traditional recipes and treat every guest like family.

As time passed, our dedication helped us develop our identity and expand our culinary offerings. From a humble beginning to a refined fine-dining concept, our journey has always been guided by our love for Indian cuisine.

While our vision continues to grow, our commitment to authentic flavours remains unchanged.
